## Rehearsal Checklist

Before filming Scene 6 (Live demonstration):
1. **Server Configuration**: Ensure `server/.env` has a valid `TINYFISH_API_KEY` and `TF_MODE=record`. Confirm the side panel displays the green active **`RECORD`** badge.
2. **Article Selection**: Select a clean news or health article (e.g. from Reuters, BBC, or CDC/WHO) where a specific statistic appears immediately next to an inline anchor link to the source.
3. **Dry Run**: Perform one complete rehearsal trace offline before starting the screen recorder:
   - Verify network latency is under 10 seconds.
   - Confirm the progress tracker steps (`Connecting…` $\to$ `Reading starting page citations…` $\to$ `Searching…`) stream clearly.
   - Confirm the result card renders properly with verdict, quotes, and checked count.
4. **Reset State**: Click **Clear page mark** in the side panel, refresh the article tab, and close/re-open the side panel to start with a clean UI.
5. **Record Smoothly**: Start the recording, highlight the target statistic, trigger trace with `Alt+Shift+T`, and allow the progress indicator to stream naturally.
